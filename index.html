About Us

  • Full roof replacements for residential & commercial properties

What We Do

Our Services

From new construction to storm repairs, Costigan handles every roofing need with precision.

Roof Replacement
Complete tear-off and replacement of existing roofing systems using premium shingles and materials built to handle Michigan winters.
Roof Repair
Fast, reliable repairs for leaks, storm damage, missing or curling shingles, flashing issues, and more. We stop problems before they worsen.
Emergency Repairs
Storm damage? Active leak? We respond quickly to protect your home from further water damage and get your roof secure again.
Roof Inspections
Thorough inspections to catch issues early — ideal before buying a home, after storms, or as part of regular maintenance to extend roof life.
General Contracting
Beyond roofing, Costigan offers broader general contracting and building services to meet a range of residential and commercial needs.
New Construction
Working with builders and homeowners on new-build roofing installations. Done right from the start, with materials and workmanship that last.
